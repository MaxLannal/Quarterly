# Seed review: catalog year 2026-27

Hand-check sheet for prompt 05. Every row is one rule with the verbatim quote it came from. Please check the quote supports the rule, and look hardest at rows marked **yes** under needs_review.

> **Status (2026-11-14): this hand check is deferred until before deployment.** Richard's decision: we are building a working demo first, and researching the catalog rule by rule takes more effort than the demo needs. Until then the seed is used as encoded. Unclear rules keep the strict reading, with a warning explaining it. Program versions stay in `review` status, shown in development through `SHOW_UNPUBLISHED_PROGRAMS=true`.

## Provenance

- **Catalog year:** 2026-2027. Every College Catalog page fetched carries the heading "2026-2027 Catalog", so no fallback year was used.
- **Fetched:** 2026-11-12 (UTC), one request at a time, as raw HTML with curl (not a summarising fetch tool) so quotes could be checked character for character.
- **Quotes:** copied from the page text with whitespace collapsed (runs of spaces, non-breaking spaces and line breaks become one space; zero-width spaces dropped). A script rejects any quote that is not found on one line of the page text, and any quote containing table separators.
- **Sources:** College Catalog pages under http://collegecatalog.uchicago.edu/thecollege/ (The Core and its nine area pages, Degree Requirements, Enrollment Policies and Statuses, Computer Science, Joint BA/MS or BS/MS in Computer Science, Mathematics, Economics, Statistics, Data Science, and the departmental pages for Arts Core and science courses); the graduate catalog pages for Computer Science, Mathematics and Statistics (graduate course blocks only); study-abroad.uchicago.edu (the two program pages and the faculty-led list); and the College's Core requirements PDF (one sentence: civilization studies abroad may be taken in the third year; the PDF carries no catalog year).
- **Generated** by scripts that read the fetched pages; data is in `data/seed/2026-27/`. Golden plans are in `data/golden/`.

## Conventions

- **Courses:** one entry per CourseLeaf block: code, title, units, description, Note(s), Equivalent Course(s) (as `crosslists`), the Prerequisite(s) sentence verbatim (`prereq_text`) and its DSL form (`prereq`), Terms Offered. Course `source.quote` is the block's title line.
- **Scope of courses:** every block on the Computer Science, Mathematics, Economics, Statistics and Data Science pages and on the Core area pages; every CMSC 30000-level block on the graduate Computer Science page (Bx/MS electives); and single courses named by a rule (Arts Core courses, science sequences, MATH 3xxxx approved courses, SPAN/FREN 10300).
- **Cross-list partners:** when a block lists an Equivalent Course that has no block on a fetched page, the partner is seeded as a copy (same title, units, prerequisite, terms) with `source.quote` = that Equivalent Course(s) line. These copies say so in `notes`.
- **Terms Offered:** read only when the line is a plain list of quarters (optionally followed by "L." or a note). Lines such as "TBD", "Not offered in 2026-2027" or year-specific remarks give no `terms_offered`.
- **Prerequisites:** sentences made only of course codes and and/or/slash connectives are formalised mechanically (`and` binds looser than `or`; a bare number reuses the previous subject). Others were encoded by hand for courses the rules name. Sentences addressed only to master's or graduate students were dropped. "or consent of instructor" becomes a `consent` alternative (warning only). Anything not formalised keeps `prereq_text`, has no `prereq`, and is marked `needs_review`.
- **Node paths** follow docs/CONTRACTS.md: program id plus child ids (`cmsc-bs/theory/theory-c`); parts of a bounded_parts node are `<node>/<part>`.
- **Policy and limit quotes.** Share limits, `max_from` caps and `policy.requires` / `policy.excludes` carry their own source in the YAML (`source` on the limit or cap, `requires_source` / `excludes_source` on the policy); `make seed` rejects any without one. They are:
